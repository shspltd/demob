Need to know

Frequently asked questions

A few quick answers to help you book with confidence.

How do I make a booking? −
Choose a listing, select your preferred date and time, enter your details and submit the booking form. A confirmation reference will be generated in this demo.
Can I change or cancel a booking? +
The full platform can support rescheduling and cancellation rules based on each provider's policy. These actions are illustrative in this frontend demo.
Are payments processed online? +
This demo does not collect payments. A production deployment can integrate a payment gateway and issue receipts or invoices.
Can businesses manage their own availability? +
Yes. A complete booking platform can provide provider dashboards for services, schedules, capacity, blackout dates and booking management.
